## Overview

**DR-RISE (Demand Response – Residential Innovation for a Sustainable Energy system)** is a Horizon Europe project focused on transforming residential energy use through advanced demand response strategies.

The project develops a **holistic residential demand response platform** that integrates:

- Real-time and predictive optimisation of household energy flows
- Coordination between end-users, aggregators, and grid operators
- User-centric design with co-creation methodologies
- Privacy-preserving and secure data management

Its goal is to unlock the potential of residential flexibility to support a more **efficient, resilient, and decarbonised European energy system**.

---

## Motivation

The European energy system is undergoing a rapid transformation driven by decarbonisation targets and increasing electrification of demand.

However, residential demand response remains underexploited due to several barriers:

- Limited engagement and awareness of end-users
- Fragmented integration of flexibility into energy markets
- Lack of scalable residential optimisation tools
- Regulatory and institutional constraints

DR-RISE addresses these challenges by placing citizens at the centre of the energy system and enabling **active participation in demand-side flexibility markets**.
