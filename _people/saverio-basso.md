bio: Researcher at IDSIA USI-SUPSI since 2021. He earned his MSc and PhD in Computer Science from the University of Milan. His doctoral research focused on data-driven techniques for generating Dantzig–Wolfe decompositions for mixed-integer problems, as well as on the design of parallel and distributed column generation algorithms. His primary research interests lie in combinatorial optimization problems, with a particular focus on exact and data-driven approaches to resource-constrained shortest path problems.
