abstract:
    "This paper describes the framework proposed by the UNIMIB Team for the
    task of Named Entity Recognition and Linking of Italian tweets (NEEL-IT). The proposed
    pipeline, which represents an entry level system, is composed of three main steps:
    (1) Named Entity Recognition using Conditional Random Fields, (2) Named Entity Linking
    by considering both Supervised and Neural-Network Language models, and (3) NIL clustering
    by using a graph-based approach."
