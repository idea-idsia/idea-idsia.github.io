abstract:
    Although the vast majority of knowledge bases KBs are heavily biased towards\nEnglish,
    Wikipedias do cover very different topics in different languages.\nExploiting this,
    we introduce a new multilingual dataset (X-WikiRE), framing\nrelation extraction
    as a multilingual machine reading problem. We show that by\nleveraging this resource
    it is possible to robustly transfer models\ncross-lingually and that multilingual
    support significantly improves\n(zero-shot) relation extraction, enabling the population
    of low-resourced KBs\nfrom their well-populated counterparts.\n
