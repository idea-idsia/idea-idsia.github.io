authors:

    - Evelina Lamma

abstract: >
  This work presents the application of artificial intelligence techniques for processing and aligning images acquired from 3D scanners, which are used to 
  generate digital three-dimensional models. To improve the alignment of frames captured in successive scans and achieve a more accurate reconstruction of 
  the 3D model, the Simulated Annealing technique was integrated with an image processing algorithm, namely the Iterative Closest Point (ICP) algorithm. 
  ICP aligns each newly acquired frame with the previously generated model through successive rotations and translations.
